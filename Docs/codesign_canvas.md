# 02. Preferred Co-Design Canvas: ED Observation Unit Triage

## 1. System Intent & Vision
To deliver an accessible, self-check-in kiosk with a real-time nurse observation dashboard, reducing ED waiting times while ensuring safe, automated ESI acuity prioritisation.

## 2. Target Users & Stakeholders
- **Primary Patient Users:** Adults, elderly patients, English and non-English speakers, walk-in emergency arrivals.
- **Primary Clinical Users:** Triage nurses, Charge nurses, ED physicians.

## 3. Accessibility & Inclusivity Guidelines
- **Visual:** High contrast mode, colorblind-friendly palettes, adjustable text sizing.
- **Auditory:** Spoken audio guidance prompts with multi-language toggle.
- **Physical:** Large touch targets for motor ease.

## 4. Ethical & Environmental Constraints
- Clear visual status cues for emergency escalations.
- No direct autonomous diagnostic decisions without clinical sign-off.
- Hygiene and cleanability considerations for kiosk physical interface.
